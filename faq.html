FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

How do I register for an event?

Open the Events page, select an event, and click Register Now.

Can I register for multiple events?

Yes. You can register for multiple events as long as seats are available.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes. You can cancel your registration from the Student Dashboard.

Do I receive a certificate?

Certificate availability depends on the individual event.

Who can participate?

Participation depends on the event. Check the event details before registering.
